2. Logging In

All users access the portal through the same login page at your school's portal URL (e.g., portal.yourschool.com/portal/login).

Login Credentials

RoleLogin WithWho Creates the Account
School AdminUsername or Email + PasswordPlatform administrator
TeacherUsername + PasswordSchool admin
StudentUsername + PasswordSchool admin
ParentUsername + PasswordSchool admin

How to Log In

  1. Go to your school's portal URL in any web browser (works on phone, tablet, or computer).
  2. If accessing from the platform domain (not a school custom domain), select your school from the dropdown.
  3. Enter your username (or email for admins).
  4. Enter your password.
  5. Click "Log in".
  6. You will be redirected to your role-specific dashboard automatically.
First-time login: If your account was just created by the admin, you may be required to change your default password. Follow the on-screen instructions to set a new password.

Forgot Your Password?

  1. Click "Forgot your password?" on the login page.
  2. Enter your email address (available for admin accounts with email).
  3. Check your email for the password reset link.
  4. Click the link and set a new password.
Students and teachers without email: If you forget your password, contact your school administrator — they can reset it from your account settings.

3. Navigating the Portal

The portal uses a responsive layout that adapts to your device:

On Desktop / Laptop

  • Sidebar (left) — Main navigation with grouped menu items. Click any item to go to that page.
  • Top bar (right) — Shows your school logo, notifications bell, and your profile dropdown menu.
  • Main area (center) — The content of the current page.

On Mobile / Tablet

  • Tap the ☰ hamburger menu (top-left) to open the sidebar navigation.
  • The sidebar slides out. Tap any menu item to navigate.
  • Tap outside the sidebar or the X button to close it.

Common Navigation Elements

  • Notifications — The bell icon in the top bar shows unread notifications (approvals, rejections, new content). Click to view all.
  • Profile Menu — Click your name/avatar in the top-right for profile settings, appearance (dark/light mode), and logout.
  • Breadcrumbs — Shown at the top of detail pages to help you navigate back.
  • Session/Term Filter — On dashboards and data pages, use the session and term dropdowns to view data for different periods.
Tip: The portal supports dark mode. Go to Settings → Appearance from your profile menu to switch between light and dark themes.
🛡️ Part A: School Administrator Guide

4.1 First-Time Setup Checklist

When your school is first set up on the platform, follow these steps in order:

  1. School Settings: Go to Management → School Settings. Set your school name, motto, email, phone, address. Upload your school logo and set brand colors.
  2. School Levels: Go to Management → School Levels. Create levels like "Nursery", "Primary", "Secondary".
  3. Classes: Go to Academics → Classes. Create classes within each level (e.g., Primary 1, Primary 2, etc.).
  4. Subjects: Go to Academics → Subjects. Create subjects (e.g., Mathematics, English) and assign them to classes.
  5. Grading Setup: Go to Scores & Reports → Grading Setup. Configure grading scales (A, B, C, etc.), score components (CA, Exam), and report card settings.
  6. Academic Session: Go to Academics → Sessions & Terms. Create a session (e.g., "2025/2026") — this auto-creates 3 terms. Activate the session and the current term.
  7. Add Teachers: Go to People → Teachers. Create teacher accounts and assign each to their class(es).
  8. Add Students: Go to People → Students. Add students one-by-one or use CSV bulk import.
  9. Add Parents (Optional): Go to People → Parents. Create parent accounts and link each to their child(ren).
Tip: Share usernames and default passwords with teachers, students, and parents after creating their accounts. Remind them to change their password on first login.

4.3 Managing Students

Sidebar: People → Students

Adding a Student

  1. Go to People → Students and click "Add Student".
  2. Fill in: Full Name, Username (unique within your school), Gender.
  3. Select the student's Level (e.g., Primary) and Class (e.g., Primary 3).
  4. Optionally fill in: Date of Birth, Admission Number, Address, Blood Group, Medical Notes.
  5. Optionally upload a student photo (stored securely in the cloud).
  6. Click "Create Student". A default password is automatically set.

Bulk Import (CSV)

  1. On the Students page, click "Import CSV".
  2. Click "Download Template" to get the CSV template file.
  3. Open the template in Excel/Google Sheets and fill in student data (one row per student).
  4. Upload the filled CSV file.
  5. Review the Preview showing which students will be imported and any errors.
  6. Click "Import" to create all valid student accounts.

Editing / Deactivating a Student

  • Edit: Click the student's name or the edit icon. Update any details, change class, upload a photo.
  • Deactivate: Click "Deactivate" to prevent the student from logging in while preserving all their data (results, scores, quiz attempts). Use this for students who leave the school.
  • Reactivate: Click "Activate" to re-enable a deactivated student.
  • Reset Password: Click "Reset Password" to generate a new default password for a student who forgot theirs.

4.4 Managing Teachers

Sidebar: People → Teachers
  1. Go to People → Teachers and click "Add Teacher".
  2. Fill in: Full Name, Username, Email (optional), Phone, Gender, Level.
  3. Select which class(es) this teacher is assigned to. Teachers can only access students in their assigned classes.
  4. Click "Create Teacher".
Important: Everything a teacher uploads (results, assignments, notices, quizzes, games, CBT exams) goes into a pending approval queue. You must approve it before students can see it.

You can also: Edit teacher details, Assign/reassign classes, Deactivate/Activate accounts, Reset passwords.

4.5 Managing Parents

Sidebar: People → Parents
  1. Go to People → Parents and click "Add Parent".
  2. Fill in: Full Name, Username, Phone, Relationship (Father/Mother/Guardian), Occupation, Address.
  3. Link to children: Select one or more students to link as this parent's children. One parent can have multiple children across different classes.
  4. Click "Create Parent".

Parents can log in and see: their children's report cards, uploaded results, assignments, quiz scores, game progress, CBT results, and school notices.

4.6 Classes & Subjects

Sidebar: Academics → Classes | Academics → Subjects

Classes

  1. Go to Academics → Classes and click "Add Class".
  2. Select the Level this class belongs to (e.g., Primary).
  3. Enter the class Name (e.g., "Primary 3").
  4. Optionally set a Capacity (max students) and assign a Class Teacher.
  5. Click "Create Class".

Subjects

  1. Go to Academics → Subjects and click "Add Subject".
  2. Enter the subject Name (e.g., "Mathematics").
  3. Click "Create Subject".

Assigning Subjects to Classes

  1. Go to Academics → Subjects and click the "Subject Assignments" tab.
  2. Select a class, then check which subjects should be taught in that class.
  3. Use "Quick Assign" to assign a subject to multiple classes at once.
  4. Click "Save".

4.7 Academic Sessions & Terms

Sidebar: Academics → Sessions & Terms

Creating a Session

  1. Go to Academics → Sessions & Terms and click "Create Session".
  2. Enter the session name (e.g., "2025/2026"), start date, and end date.
  3. Click "Create". Three terms (First, Second, Third) are automatically created.

Activating a Session/Term

  • Only one session and one term can be "current" at a time.
  • Click "Set as Current" on the session you want active.
  • Expand the session to see its terms, then click "Activate" on the current term.
  • The active session/term determines which period data is shown by default across the portal.

Editing Terms

Click the edit icon on any term to set its start and end dates or change its status (upcoming, active, completed).

4.8 Score Entry & Report Cards

Sidebar: Scores & Reports → Score Entry | Scores & Reports → Report Cards

Entering Scores

  1. Go to Scores & Reports → Score Entry.
  2. Select the Session, Term, Class, and Subject.
  3. A table appears with all students in that class. Enter scores for each score component (e.g., CA1, CA2, Exam) as configured in Grading Setup.
  4. Click "Save Scores". Scores are saved and can be edited until locked.
  5. Use "Lock Scores" to prevent further editing once finalized.

Generating Report Cards

  1. Go to Scores & Reports → Report Cards.
  2. Select the Session, Term, and optionally a specific Class.
  3. Click "Generate Reports". The system compiles scores for all subjects into report cards.
  4. Review generated report cards — you can see each student's overall performance.
  5. Approve report cards individually or use "Bulk Approve" to approve all at once.
  6. Publish approved report cards to make them visible to students and parents.

Downloading & Exporting

  • Download PDF: Click the download icon on any report card to get a printable PDF.
  • Download All: Use "Download All" to get a ZIP file of all report cards for a class.
  • Export CSV: Export score data to CSV for use in spreadsheets.

Editing Report Card Data

After generation, you can edit additional report card fields (teacher comments, principal remarks, attendance data, etc.) by clicking "Edit Data" on individual report cards or using the "Bulk Edit" feature.

4.9 Uploaded Results (PDFs)

Sidebar: Scores & Reports → Uploaded Results

In addition to generated report cards, you can upload traditional result PDFs (scanned results, external reports).

Single Upload

  1. Go to Scores & Reports → Uploaded Results and click "Upload Result".
  2. Select the Student, Session, Term, and Class.
  3. Upload the PDF file (max 10MB).
  4. Click "Upload". Results you upload are approved immediately.

Bulk Upload

  1. Click "Bulk Upload".
  2. Select the class, session, and term.
  3. Upload multiple PDF files at once. Name each file as the student's username (e.g., john_doe.pdf).
  4. The system auto-matches files to students by filename.
  5. Review the preview, then click "Upload All".

4.10 Assignments

Sidebar: Academics → Assignments
  1. Go to Academics → Assignments and click "Add Assignment".
  2. Select the Class, Session, Term, and Week Number (1–12).
  3. Enter a Title and optional description.
  4. Upload the assignment file (PDF, DOC, etc.).
  5. Optionally set a Due Date.
  6. Click "Create". Assignments you create are approved immediately.

4.11 CBT (Computer-Based Testing)

Sidebar: CBT & Interactive → CBT (tabs: Exams, Assessments, Assignments)

CBT allows you to create online exams, assessments, and assignments that students take directly on the portal.

Three CBT Categories

CategoryPurposeExamples
ExamFormal examinationsEnd-of-term exam, mid-term exam
AssessmentContinuous assessment testsCA Test 1, CA Test 2, pop quiz
AssignmentHomework / take-home workWeekly homework, research project

Creating a CBT Exam

  1. Go to CBT & Interactive → CBT. Click the "Create" dropdown and choose the category.
  2. Fill in: Title, Class, Subject (you can create new subjects inline), Session, Term.
  3. Set optional: Time Limit, Pass Mark %, Max Attempts, Due Date.
  4. Optionally link to a Score Component (e.g., "CA1") so CBT scores feed into report cards.
  5. Choose to add questions manually or use AI generation.

AI Generation

  1. Select "AI Generate" mode.
  2. Upload a source document (PDF/DOC, up to 10MB) or paste a link (Google Docs, website).
  3. Choose: Number of questions (5–30), Difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard), Question types (MCQ, True/False, Fill Blank, Short Answer, Theory, Matching).
  4. Click "Generate Questions". The AI creates questions from your content.
  5. Review and edit each generated question — you can modify text, options, correct answers, and explanations.
  6. Each AI generation costs 1 credit.

Manual Creation

Add questions one by one — choose question type, enter the question text, options, correct answer, points, and optional explanation. Manual creation is always free (no credits).

Publishing & Monitoring

  • Publish: Click "Publish" to make the exam available to students.
  • Monitor: View real-time exam progress — see which students are taking it, who has finished, live submission count.
  • Results: After students submit, view results with scores, pass/fail status, and time taken.
  • Analytics: View question-level analytics — which questions were hardest, score distribution.
  • Grading: For theory/short-answer questions, grade manually (individual or bulk grading).
  • Export: Export results to CSV. Bulk export all CBT results at once.

4.15 Teacher Approvals

Sidebar: Management → Approvals

When teachers upload results, assignments, notices, quizzes, games, or CBT exams, these go into a pending approval queue.

Teacher submits content → Status: "Pending" ↓ Admin reviews in Approvals queue ↓ [Approve] → Content becomes visible to students/parents [Reject] → Teacher notified with reason, can re-submit

How to Review

  1. Go to Management → Approvals.
  2. You'll see all pending items grouped by type (results, assignments, notices, quizzes, games).
  3. Click "View" to preview the content (view the PDF, read the notice, preview quiz questions).
  4. Click "Approve" to make it visible, or "Reject" to send it back with a reason.
Note: Your own uploads (as admin) are auto-approved and visible immediately — they skip the approval queue.

4.16 Promoting Students

Sidebar: Management → Promotions
  1. Go to Management → Promotions.
  2. Select the Source Class (where students are now, e.g., Primary 1).
  3. Select the Destination Class (where they'll go, e.g., Primary 2).
  4. Click "Preview" to see the list of students who will be promoted.
  5. Uncheck any students who should NOT be promoted (e.g., repeating students).
  6. Click "Promote" to move all selected students to the new class.
Before promoting: Make sure you've created the next academic session and that all current-term data (scores, results) is finalized.

Branding

  • Colors: Set Primary, Secondary, and Accent colors. These customize the portal's look to match your school identity.

Portal Settings

  • Enable/Disable Parent Portal

4.18 AI Credits

Sidebar: Management → AI Credits

AI credits power quiz, game, and CBT question generation. Your school gets 15 free credits per month (reset on the 1st). Each AI generation costs 1 credit. Manual creation is always free.

Purchasing Credits

  1. Go to Management → AI Credits and click "Purchase Credits".
  2. Select the number of credits (multiples of 5). Price: ₦1,000 per 5 credits (₦200/credit).
  3. Complete payment via Paystack.
  4. Credits are added to your balance immediately. Purchased credits never expire.

Allocating Credits to Levels

Optionally allocate credits to specific levels (e.g., give Primary 20 credits, Nursery 10 credits). Teachers see their level's remaining credits. If no allocation is set, all teachers draw from the shared school pool.

4.19 Grading Setup

Sidebar: Scores & Reports → Grading Setup

Configure how your school grades students:

Grading Scales

Define grade boundaries (e.g., A = 70–100, B = 60–69, etc.) with labels, colors, and remarks. You can create multiple scales if needed.

Score Components

Define what makes up a student's total score (e.g., CA1 = 20 marks, CA2 = 20 marks, Exam = 60 marks). Components must total 100%.

Report Card Settings

Configure what appears on generated report cards: school name, logo, principal's signature text, whether to show position/ranking, term summary fields, etc.

4.20 Audit Logs

Sidebar: Management → Audit Logs

Every significant action in the portal is logged — creating students, uploading results, changing settings, promotions, etc. Use audit logs to:

  • Track who did what and when
  • Investigate issues or disputes
  • Monitor teacher activity
  • View IP addresses and timestamps

6.5 Taking CBT Exams

Sidebar: CBT & Interactive → CBT (tabs: Exams, Assessments, Assignments)

CBT exams are organized into three tabs: Exams, Assessments, and Assignments.

Before Starting

  1. Go to CBT & Interactive → CBT and click the appropriate tab.
  2. You'll see Available exams (ready to take), Completed exams (already taken), and Upcoming exams (not yet available).
  3. Click "View Details" on an available exam to see: number of questions, total points, time limit, pass mark, attempts remaining, and rules.
  4. Click "Start Exam" and confirm in the modal.

During the Exam

  • Timer — Counts down in the header. Turns amber at 5 minutes, red at 1 minute.
  • Navigation dots — Numbered circles at the bottom. Green = answered, gray = unanswered, orange = flagged for review.
  • Flag for review — Click the flag icon on any question to mark it for later review.
  • Question types — Multiple choice (click an option), True/False, Fill in the blank (type answer), Short answer (text area), Theory (long text area).
  • Auto-save — Your answers are saved automatically as you go.
  • Navigation — Use "Previous" and "Next" buttons, or click any navigation dot to jump to a question.

Submitting

  • Click "Submit Exam" when finished. You'll see a confirmation showing how many questions are answered/unanswered.
  • If the timer runs out, the exam is auto-submitted.
  • After submission, you'll see your results — score, percentage, pass/fail status, and (if enabled) correct answers with explanations.
Anti-cheating: Tab-switching may be monitored. The exam may go fullscreen. Right-click and text selection may be disabled during the exam.

6.6 Taking Quizzes

8. Security & Account Management

Passwords

  • All passwords are securely encrypted (bcrypt). No one — not even the admin — can see your password.
  • Change your password regularly from Settings → Security.
  • If you forget your password:
    • Admins with email: Use "Forgot password" on the login page.
    • Everyone else: Ask your school admin to reset it.

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

  1. Go to Settings → Security.
  2. Click "Enable Two-Factor Authentication".
  3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.).
  4. Enter the verification code to confirm.
  5. Save your recovery codes in a safe place — these allow you to log in if you lose access to your authenticator app.

Session Security

  • Sessions automatically timeout after a period of inactivity (default: 30 minutes).
  • Always click "Logout" when you're done, especially on shared devices.
  • Login attempts are rate-limited to prevent brute-force attacks.

Deactivated Accounts

If your account has been deactivated, you'll see a message on the login page. Contact your school administrator to have it reactivated.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

General

Q: Which browsers are supported?

The portal works on all modern browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge. We recommend keeping your browser up to date.

Q: Can I use the portal on my phone?

Yes! The portal is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and computers.

Q: What is the URL for my school's portal?

Your school admin will provide this. It's typically portal.yourschool.com or you can access it via the platform URL.

For School Admins

Q: How do I change my school's logo and colors?

Go to Management → School Settings → Branding. Upload your logo and set your brand colors.

Q: Can I import students from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Go to People → Students → "Import CSV". Download the template, fill it in, and upload.

Q: What happens to a student's data when I deactivate them?

All data (results, scores, quiz attempts) is preserved. The student simply can't log in. You can reactivate them at any time.

Q: How do I purchase more AI credits?

Go to Management → AI Credits → "Purchase Credits". Credits are ₦200 each, sold in multiples of 5.

Q: Can teachers publish content directly without my approval?

By default, no — all teacher content requires approval. You can disable this requirement in School Settings → Portal → Teacher Approval.

For Teachers

Q: Why can't I see all students?

You can only see students in classes assigned to you. Ask your admin to assign you to additional classes if needed.

Q: My submission was rejected. What do I do?

Check Tracking → My Submissions for the rejection reason. Make the necessary changes and re-submit.

Q: Can I create quizzes without using AI credits?

Yes! Choose "Manual" creation mode when creating quizzes, games, or CBT exams. Manual creation is always free.

For Students

Q: My exam timer ran out! What happens?

Your exam is automatically submitted with whatever answers you've provided. All auto-saved answers are included.

Q: Can I retake a quiz or exam?

Only if the teacher set it to allow multiple attempts. Check the "Attempts" count on the quiz/exam card.

Q: I can't see my report card. Why?

Report cards must be generated, approved, and published by your admin before they appear. Check back later or ask your teacher.

For Parents

Q: I can't see one of my children. Why?

Your account must be linked to each child by the school admin. Contact them to link additional children.

Q: Can I communicate with teachers through the portal?

This feature is planned for a future update. Currently, please contact teachers through the school's regular channels.
