1. Your Role on DX-SchoolPortal

As a teacher, DX-SchoolPortal gives you a dedicated workspace to manage everything for your assigned class(es) — all from one place, on any device.

What You Can Do

FeatureWhat You Can DoApproval Required?
Score EntryEnter and edit student scores for your classesNo (admin can lock)
Report CardsGenerate, view, download, and add commentsSubmit to admin for approval
Uploaded Results (PDFs)Upload result PDFs for students in your classesYes
AssignmentsUpload class assignments by weekYes
CBT Exams & AssessmentsCreate, publish, monitor, and grade online examsYes
AI QuizzesCreate quizzes (AI-generated or manual)Yes
Educational GamesCreate interactive games for studentsYes
NoticesPost announcements to students/parentsYes
Student InsightsView analytics for students in your classesNo
Key rule: Everything you upload or create goes into a pending approval queue. Your school administrator reviews it and either approves (making it visible to students) or rejects it (with a reason for you to address). You will be notified of the outcome.

Your Scope: Your Classes Only

You can only see and act on students in the classes assigned to you by your administrator. If you believe you should have access to additional classes, contact your administrator.

2. Logging In

Open your school's portal URL in any web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge — works on phone, tablet, or computer).

  1. Navigate to your school's portal URL (e.g., portal.yourschool.com/portal/login).
  2. Enter your Username — provided by your school administrator. Teachers log in with username only (not email).
  3. Enter your Password.
  4. Click "Log in". You'll be taken directly to your teacher dashboard.
First login: If the admin just created your account, you may be required to change the default password. Follow the on-screen prompts to set your new password.

Forgot Your Password?

Teachers typically don't have email linked to their accounts. If you forget your password, contact your school administrator — they can reset it from your profile in the admin panel.

Logging Out

Always log out when done, especially on shared or school computers. Click your name/avatar in the top-right corner and select "Log out". Sessions also auto-expire after a period of inactivity.

3. Navigating the Portal

On Desktop / Laptop

  • Sidebar (left) — Main navigation, grouped into sections: Teaching, Scores & Reports, Content, CBT & Interactive, Communications, Tracking, and Settings.
  • Top bar — Shows your school logo, a notifications bell (for approval results), and your profile menu (top-right).
  • Main area — Page content.

On Mobile / Tablet

  • Tap the ☰ hamburger icon (top-left) to open the navigation sidebar.
  • Tap any menu item to navigate, then tap outside the sidebar to close it.

Key UI Elements

  • Notifications Bell — Alerts you when submissions are approved or rejected by the admin. Click to view details.
  • Session & Term Dropdowns — Appear on most data pages. Switch between periods to view or enter data for different terms.
  • Breadcrumbs — At the top of detail pages so you can easily navigate back.
  • Dark Mode — Available from Settings → Appearance.

4. Your Dashboard

Sidebar: Teaching → Dashboard

Your dashboard gives an at-a-glance view of your teaching workspace:

  • Welcome banner — Greeting, your name, current session and term.
  • Rejected submissions alert — Prominently shown if any of your recent uploads were rejected by the admin. It displays the rejection reason so you know exactly what to fix and re-submit.
  • Statistics cards:
    • Students in your assigned classes
    • Pending submissions (awaiting admin approval)
    • Published content (approved and live)
    • Active CBT exams
    • Published quizzes and games
  • Quick Actions — Shortcut buttons for common tasks: Upload Result, Add Assignment, Create CBT, Create Quiz.
  • Session/Term filter — Use the dropdowns at the top to switch between different academic periods.
Tip: Check your dashboard at the start of each day. Any rejected content from the admin will be clearly highlighted here, reminding you to address and re-submit it.

5. Understanding the Approval Workflow

Everything you create or upload — results, assignments, CBT exams, quizzes, games, and notices — goes through an approval process before students or parents can see it.

You create/upload content ↓ Status: ⏳ Pending Approval ↓ Admin reviews your submission ↓ ┌────────────────┬────────────────────────────────┐ │ ✅ APPROVED │ ❌ REJECTED │ │ │ │ │ Content is │ You receive a notification │ │ immediately │ with the rejection reason. │ │ visible to │ Edit/fix the content and │ │ students and │ re-submit. │ │ parents. │ │ └────────────────┴────────────────────────────────┘

Checking Approval Status

You can always check the status of all your submissions in one place:

Sidebar: Tracking → My Submissions

Each item shows one of three statuses:

  • Pending — Awaiting admin review. Students cannot see it yet.
  • Approved — Live and visible to students/parents.
  • Rejected — Read the rejection reason and re-submit with the necessary corrections.
Note: Your school admin may have disabled the approval requirement for some or all content types. If that is the case, your content will be auto-approved and published immediately. Ask your admin if you're unsure.

6. Your Students

6.1 Viewing Your Class List

Sidebar: Teaching → My Students

This page lists all students in the classes assigned to you. For each student you can see:

  • Full name and username
  • Class and level
  • Profile photo (if uploaded)
  • Gender and admission number

You can search by name and filter by class if you teach multiple classes. You cannot edit student details — only your administrator can do that.

6.2 Student Insights & Analytics

Sidebar: Tracking → Student Insights

View performance analytics for your students:

  • Score trends across subjects and terms
  • CBT exam performance (scores, attempts, pass/fail rates)
  • Quiz results and average scores
  • Game activity and progress
  • Assignments available vs. downloaded

Use this to identify students who may need extra attention and to gauge overall class performance.

7. Score Entry & Report Cards

Sidebar: Scores & Reports → Score Entry | Scores & Reports → Report Cards

7.1 Entering Scores

  1. Go to Scores & Reports → Score Entry.
  2. Select the Session and Term from the dropdowns at the top.
  3. Select your Class (e.g., Primary 3) and the Subject (e.g., Mathematics).
  4. A score sheet table appears with all students in that class as rows, and the score components as columns (e.g., CA1, CA2, Exam — as set up by your administrator).
  5. Click on any cell and type the student's score for that component. Press Tab to move to the next cell.
  6. Click "Save Scores" to save your entries. You can save partially and return later to complete it.
Tips for faster entry:
  • Use Tab to move forward through cells, Shift+Tab to go back.
  • Leave a cell blank if a student was absent or has no score yet — blank is not the same as zero.
  • Scores are validated against the maximum for each component. You'll see an error if you enter more than the allowed maximum.

Locking Scores

Once you're satisfied that scores for a subject are final, you (or the admin) can click "Lock Scores" to prevent further changes. This is typically done after the term ends. Locked scores are shown with a lock icon; ask your admin to unlock if you need to make corrections.

7.2 Generating Report Cards

  1. Go to Scores & Reports → Report Cards.
  2. Select the Session, Term, and Class.
  3. Click "Generate Reports". The system compiles all subject scores for each student into their report card automatically.
  4. Review the generated report cards — check each student's totals, grades, and position.
  5. Click "Edit Data" on a report card to add:
    • Your class teacher's comment
    • Attendance data (days present/absent)
    • Any other custom fields your school uses
  6. Use "Bulk Edit" to add comments or data to all report cards at once.
  7. When ready, click "Bulk Submit" to send all report cards to the admin for approval and publishing.
Important: Students and parents cannot see report cards until they are approved and published by the administrator. Submit them as early as possible to give the admin time to review.

7.3 Exporting & Downloading

  • Download PDF: Click the download icon on any individual report card to get a printable PDF.
  • Download All (ZIP): Download all report cards for a class as a single ZIP file of PDFs.
  • Export CSV: Export raw score data to a spreadsheet for your own records.

8. Uploading Results (PDFs)

Sidebar: Content → Uploaded Results

In addition to system-generated report cards, you can upload traditional result PDF files (scanned result sheets, externally generated reports, etc.) for individual students in your classes.

8.1 Uploading a Result

  1. Go to Content → Uploaded Results and click "Upload Result".
  2. Select the Student from the dropdown (only students in your classes appear).
  3. Select the Session and Term this result is for.
  4. Click "Choose File" and select the PDF from your device. Maximum file size: 10MB.
  5. Click "Upload".

The result is now in your pending queue. The administrator will review and approve it. Once approved, the student and their parent(s) can view the PDF securely.

8.2 Checking the Status of Uploaded Results

On the Uploaded Results page, you can see all your uploaded results and their current status:

  • Pending — Awaiting admin approval.
  • Approved — Visible to the student and parent.
  • Rejected — The admin sent it back. View the reason and re-upload the correct file.
Security: Result PDFs are stored securely in the cloud and accessed only through time-limited signed links. Students and parents cannot share the direct link with others.

9. Assignments

Sidebar: Content → Assignments

Upload weekly assignments for your classes so students can access them from their portal.

Creating an Assignment

  1. Go to Content → Assignments and click "Add Assignment".
  2. Select the Class, Session, Term, and Week Number (1–12, corresponding to the week of term).
  3. Enter an Assignment Title (e.g., "Week 3 Mathematics Homework").
  4. Optionally enter a Description with instructions for students.
  5. Upload the assignment file (PDF, Word document, image, etc.).
  6. Optionally set a Due Date — students will see overdue assignments highlighted in red.
  7. Click "Create".

The assignment goes to Pending Approval. Once the admin approves it, it appears in the relevant students' portal.

Editing or Deleting an Assignment

  • You can edit an assignment (change the file, title, or due date) while it is still Pending or if it was Rejected.
  • Once Approved, contact your admin to make changes.
  • You can delete your own pending or rejected assignments.
Tip: Be consistent with week numbers. Week 1 is the first week of term, Week 12 is the last. Students filter assignments by week, so using the correct number helps them find the right assignment quickly.

10. CBT (Computer-Based Testing)

Sidebar: CBT & Interactive → CBT

CBT allows you to create online exams, assessments, and assignments that students take directly on the portal. Results are automatically scored for objective questions.

10.1 Three CBT Categories

CategoryPurposeExamples
ExamFormal examinationsEnd-of-term exam, mid-term exam, mock exam
AssessmentContinuous assessment testsCA Test 1, CA Test 2, pop quiz, class test
AssignmentHomework / take-home workWeekly homework, research project, exercises

Each category has its own tab on the CBT page. The creation process is identical for all three.

10.2 Creating a CBT Exam

  1. Go to CBT & Interactive → CBT and click the tab for the category you want (Exams, Assessments, or Assignments).
  2. Click "Create".
  3. Fill in the basic details:
    • Title — e.g., "Mid-Term Mathematics Exam"
    • Class — Select from your assigned classes
    • Subject — Select existing, or type to create a new subject inline
    • Session & Term
  4. Configure settings:
    • Time Limit — Minutes allowed for the exam (leave blank for no limit)
    • Pass Mark % — Minimum score to pass (e.g., 50)
    • Max Attempts — How many times a student can retake (usually 1 for exams)
    • Due Date — When the exam closes (optional)
    • Shuffle Questions — Randomise question order for each student
    • Shuffle Options — Randomise answer option order
    • Show Correct Answers — Whether students see correct answers after submission
  5. Optionally link to a Score Component (e.g., "CA1") so that CBT scores feed directly into report cards.
  6. Click "Next" or "Save & Add Questions".

10.3 Adding Questions Manually

Click "Add Question" and choose the question type:

Question TypeDescriptionAuto-scored?
Multiple Choice (MCQ)4 options, one correct answer✅ Yes
True / FalseTwo options: True or False✅ Yes
Fill in the BlankStudent types a word or phrase✅ Yes (exact match)
Short AnswerBrief text response (1–2 sentences)❌ Manual grading
Theory / EssayLong-form written answer❌ Manual grading
MatchingMatch items from two columns✅ Yes

For each question, fill in:

  • Question text — The question itself
  • Answer options (for MCQ/True-False/Matching)
  • Correct answer — Mark which option is correct
  • Points — Score for a correct answer (default: 1)
  • Explanation (optional) — Shown to students after submission if correct answers are enabled

Click "Add Another Question" to continue adding, or "Done" when finished.

10.4 AI Question Generation

Instead of typing questions manually, you can use AI to generate questions from your lesson content. This costs 1 credit per generation.

  1. On the question creation page, click "AI Generate".
  2. Choose your source:
    • Upload Document — Upload a PDF, Word document, or image of your lesson notes, textbook chapter, or any printed material (up to 10MB).
    • Paste Link — Provide a Google Docs link, website URL, or online resource URL.
  3. Configure generation settings:
    • Number of questions: 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30
    • Difficulty: Easy, Medium, or Hard
    • Question types: Select which types you want (MCQ, True/False, Fill Blank, Short Answer, Theory, Matching)
  4. Click "Generate Questions". The AI reads your content and creates questions.
  5. Review every question carefully. You can:
    • Edit the question text
    • Change answer options
    • Mark the correct answer
    • Adjust point values
    • Add or edit the explanation
    • Delete any question you don't want
    • Add additional manual questions
  6. If you need more questions, click "Generate More" (costs another credit).
  7. Click "Save Questions" when satisfied.
Always review AI-generated questions! AI can occasionally produce inaccurate questions or have the wrong correct answer. You are responsible for the accuracy of questions your students see.

10.5 Publishing & Monitoring

After saving your CBT exam, it has Pending status. Once the administrator approves it:

  1. Find the exam on the CBT page (it now shows as Approved).
  2. Click "Publish" to make it available to students. Students will immediately see it on their CBT page.
  3. To monitor the exam in real time, click "Monitor" to see:
    • Which students have started the exam
    • Which students have submitted
    • Live submission count
    • Time remaining for students still in-progress
  4. You can "Unpublish" at any time to close the exam before its due date.

10.6 Viewing Results

After students submit:

  1. Click "Results" on the exam card.
  2. See a summary: average score, pass rate, score distribution.
  3. View individual student results: score, percentage, pass/fail, time taken, number of attempts.
  4. Click a student's result to see their answer for each question (and whether it was correct).
  5. View question analytics: which questions had the highest wrong-answer rate.
  6. Click "Export CSV" to download all results as a spreadsheet.

10.7 Grading Theory / Short Answer Questions

For questions that require manual grading (Short Answer, Theory/Essay), you must grade them yourself:

  1. On the results page, click "Grade".
  2. You'll see each student's written answer for each theory/short-answer question.
  3. Assign a score (between 0 and the question's maximum points).
  4. You can also add a comment/feedback for the student.
  5. For faster grading, use "Bulk Grade" — see all students' answers to the same question side by side.
  6. Click "Save Grades". The student's total score updates automatically.

11. AI Quizzes

Sidebar: CBT & Interactive → Quizzes

Quizzes are lighter-weight, primarily multiple-choice assessments — great for revision, formative assessment, or fun challenges. Unlike full CBT exams, quizzes are quicker to create and take.

11.1 Creating a Quiz

  1. Go to CBT & Interactive → Quizzes and click "Create Quiz".
  2. Choose your creation method: AI Generate or Manual.
  3. Set the quiz details:
    • Title
    • Class
    • Session & Term
    • Time Limit (optional)
    • Passing Score % (e.g., 60)
    • Max Attempts (how many times students can retake)
    • Shuffle Questions / Options
    • Show Correct Answers after submission

11.2 AI Generation (From Document or Prompt)

Costs 1 credit per generation. You get the same AI generation experience as CBT:

  1. Choose "From Document" — upload a PDF, Word doc, or image of your lesson notes — or choose "From Prompt" — type what you want, e.g. "Generate 10 multiple choice questions on the water cycle for Primary 5".
  2. Select: number of questions, question types (MCQ, True/False, Fill Blank), and difficulty.
  3. Click "Generate".
  4. Review and edit all generated questions carefully.
  5. Click "Save". The quiz goes to Pending Approval.

11.3 Manual Creation (Free — No Credits)

  1. Choose "Manual" as the creation method.
  2. Click "Add Question".
  3. Enter the question text, choose the type, add answer options, mark the correct answer, and optionally add an explanation.
  4. Repeat for each question.
  5. Click "Save".

Manual creation has no limit — you can create as many quizzes with as many questions as you want at no cost.

11.4 Viewing Quiz Results

After the quiz is published and students attempt it:

  • Click "View Results" on the quiz card.
  • See class stats: average score, highest/lowest score, pass rate.
  • View a score distribution chart.
  • See each student's score, percentage, pass/fail, and time taken.
  • See which questions were answered incorrectly most often.
  • Click "Export CSV" for a downloadable results file.

12. Educational Games

Sidebar: CBT & Interactive → Games

Create fun, educational games that students play from their portal. Games reinforce learning through engagement and healthy competition.

12.1 Game Types Explained

Game TypeHow Students Play ItBest ForAI Generates
Memory Match Flip cards to find matching term–definition pairs. Fewer moves = better score. Vocabulary, science terms, history facts 8–15 term/definition pairs
Word Scramble Unscramble jumbled letters to spell key terms. 3 hints available. Spelling, vocabulary, key concepts 10–20 words with hints
Quiz Race Rapid-fire MCQ questions, 10 seconds each. Score = speed × accuracy. Class leaderboard! Revision, exam prep, competitive review 15–25 MCQ questions
Flashcard Study Swipe through flashcards. Self-rate each as "Got it" or "Review again". Tracks mastery. Self-paced study, pre-exam revision 15–30 flashcard pairs

12.2 Creating a Game

  1. Go to CBT & Interactive → Games and click "Create Game".
  2. Select the Game Type (Memory Match, Word Scramble, Quiz Race, or Flashcard Study).
  3. Choose your creation method:
    • AI Generate (from document) — Upload lesson notes or a document. AI extracts relevant content and generates game data. Costs 1 credit.
    • AI Generate (from prompt) — Type a description, e.g. "Create a memory match game about Nigerian states and capitals for Primary 4". Costs 1 credit.
    • Manual — Type in all content yourself. Always free, no limits.
  4. Fill in details:
    • Title
    • Class
    • Session & Term
    • Difficulty: Easy, Medium, Hard
    • Time Limit (optional)
  5. Review and edit the generated/entered game content (pairs, words, questions, or cards).
  6. Click "Save". The game goes to Pending Approval.

12.3 Viewing Game Stats

After the game is published and students play it:

  • Click "View Stats" on the game card.
  • See: total play count, average score, completion rate.
  • For Quiz Race: view the class leaderboard with ranked scores.
  • See per-student play history — how many times each student played and their best score.

13. Posting Notices

Sidebar: Communications → Notices

Post announcements for students and/or parents — upcoming events, class updates, important reminders, etc.

  1. Go to Communications → Notices and click "Create Notice".
  2. Enter a Title (e.g., "Sports Day — Friday, 14 March").
  3. Enter the Content — the full text of your notice.
  4. Optionally upload an Image (a photo, poster, or banner for the notice).
  5. Choose Target Levels — leave blank to target all levels, or select specific ones (e.g., only Primary).
  6. Choose Target Roles — leave blank for all, or select Students, Parents, or both.
  7. Optionally set an Expiry Date — the notice will automatically stop appearing after this date.
  8. Click "Create". The notice goes to Pending Approval.
After approval: You can publish/unpublish your approved notices at any time from the Notices page. You can also edit notice details before re-submitting if rejected.

14. Tracking All Your Submissions

Sidebar: Tracking → My Submissions

This is your central view of everything you've submitted — results, assignments, CBT exams, quizzes, games, and notices — and their current approval status.

  • Filter by content type (results / assignments / CBT / quizzes / games / notices).
  • Filter by status (Pending / Approved / Rejected).
  • For Rejected items, click to see the rejection reason from the admin.
  • Edit and re-submit rejected items directly from this page.
Tip: Bookmark the "My Submissions" page. Checking it regularly means you'll catch rejections quickly and can re-submit before deadlines.

15. AI Credits

AI credits are used whenever you generate questions or game content using the AI feature. Manual creation is always free — AI is optional.

How Credits Work

  • 1 credit = 1 AI generation (one quiz, one CBT question set, or one game).
  • "Generate More" for an existing quiz or CBT also costs 1 credit.
  • Your school gets 15 free credits per month, reset on the 1st. Your administrator may also purchase extra credits.
  • Credits are shown on the creation page when you select AI Generate.

Running Out of Credits?

If your school or level has no credits left:

  • You can still create everything manually — no limits.
  • Contact your administrator to purchase more credits (only they can do this).
  • Free credits reset on the 1st of next month.
Note: Your admin may allocate a credit budget to your level (e.g., Primary gets 20 credits per month). The credits display on the creation page will reflect your level's available balance.

16. Security & Account Settings

Changing Your Password

  1. Click your name/avatar in the top-right corner.
  2. Select "Settings".
  3. Go to the "Security" tab.
  4. Enter your current password, then your new password twice to confirm.
  5. Click "Update Password".

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

For added security, enable 2FA from Settings → Security → "Enable Two-Factor Authentication". You'll need an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.). Save the recovery codes in a safe place.

Session Security

  • Sessions auto-expire after a period of inactivity. You'll be redirected to the login page.
  • Always log out when using a shared or school computer.

Profile Settings

From Settings → Profile, you can update your display name and (if applicable) email address. Class assignments and other school data are managed by your administrator.

17. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why can't I see certain students?

You can only see students in classes your administrator has assigned to you. If you should have access to other classes, ask your admin to update your class assignments.

Q: My uploaded result was rejected. What do I do?

Go to Tracking → My Submissions and click on the rejected item to read the admin's rejection reason. Fix the issue (e.g., wrong student selected, corrupted file) and upload again.

Q: Can I publish content directly without admin approval?

Only if your administrator has turned off the approval requirement in school settings. By default, approval is required. Check with your admin.

Q: Can I create quizzes and games without AI credits?

Yes. Choose "Manual" creation mode for quizzes, games, and CBT exams. Manual creation is always free with no limits.

Q: A student is missing from the score entry table. Why?

The student may have been deactivated or moved to a different class by the admin. Check the student list in Teaching → My Students and confirm with your administrator.

Q: The CBT exam auto-submitted for a student. Will their answers be saved?

Yes. All answers are auto-saved continuously. When time expires, whatever has been answered is automatically submitted. No answers are lost.

Q: Can I see results from previous terms?

Yes. Use the Session and Term dropdowns on any data page to switch to a previous period and view historical data.

Q: What file types can I upload for assignments and results?

PDF is preferred for results. For assignments, most document formats are accepted (PDF, DOC/DOCX, images). Maximum file size is 10MB.

Q: Can I edit a quiz after it's been published?

Published quizzes with student attempts cannot be edited (it would invalidate existing scores). Unpublish the quiz first, then make changes. If already approved, contact your admin.

Q: How do I report a portal issue or get help?

Contact your school administrator. They can escalate technical issues to the platform support team.
